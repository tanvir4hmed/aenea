# Safety and Claims

Aenea coordinates information; it does not certify that an emergency exists or that a person is safe.

- Inputs may be simulated, incomplete, stale, or wrong and are labeled by provenance.
- Generative-model output is schema-constrained advice, never direct actuator authority.
- Deterministic policy and explicit confirmation guard high-impact actions.
- The prototype does not unlock doors, disable alarms, diagnose illness, or dispatch responders.
- Users must follow official alarms and local emergency guidance.
- Demo data contains no real household security footage or personal health information.

Security or safety issues should be reported through the process in `SECURITY.md`.
